Bio

My name is Yufeng Gu. I am a PhD student in Computer Engineering at University of Michigan, advised by Reetuparna Das. Previously, I obtained my Bachelor degree in Information Engineering from Zhejiang University, P. R. China in 2020. I did my Bachelor thesis project at Yale University, working with Xiaoxiao Li and James Duncan. In summer 2019, I worked as an undergraduate student intern in PARSA at EPFL, advised by Babak Falsafi.

I am currently working on computer architecture, with a focus on domain specific accelerator and reconfigurable architecture.
